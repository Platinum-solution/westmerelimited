Referrals

Making a referral to Westmere Care

We aim to make referrals as straightforward as possible for social workers and commissioning teams, while keeping information about the young person secure at every stage.

Start an Enquiry Call Us

Our referral process

A typical referral follows these steps. Timescales can be adjusted for urgent placements — please call us directly if a decision is needed quickly.

  1. Initial enquiry

    You contact us by phone or via the form below with a brief, non-identifying overview of the placement need (age, gender, general needs, timescale).

  2. Information sharing

    If we may be able to help, we agree a secure method with you to share the child's placement information, risk assessment and care plan.

  3. Assessment and matching

    We assess the referral against our Statement of Purpose and current placement group, to check we can safely meet the young person's needs.

  4. Placement agreement

    We confirm suitability, agree terms with the placing authority, and arrange introductions where appropriate.

  5. Placement start and review

    The young person moves in with an individual placement plan, reviewed regularly with the local authority and other professionals involved.

Please do not submit child-identifying information through this website

The form below is for an initial, general enquiry only. Please do not include a child's name, date of birth, or other identifying or sensitive information. Once we have made contact, we will agree a secure route (phone or a protected email/portal) for sharing placement paperwork in line with data protection requirements.
