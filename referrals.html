Referrals

Making a referral to Westmere Care

We aim to make referrals as straightforward as possible for social workers and commissioning teams, while keeping information about the young person secure at every stage.

Start an Enquiry Call Us

Our referral process

A typical referral follows these steps. Timescales can be adjusted for urgent placements — please call us directly if a decision is needed quickly.

  1. Initial enquiry

    You contact us by phone or via the form below with a brief, non-identifying overview of the placement need (age, gender, general needs, timescale).

  2. Information sharing

    If we may be able to help, we agree a secure method with you to share the child's placement information, risk assessment and care plan.

  3. Assessment and matching

    We assess the referral against our Statement of Purpose and current placement group, to check we can safely meet the young person's needs.

  4. Placement agreement

    We confirm suitability, agree terms with the placing authority, and arrange introductions where appropriate.

  5. Placement start and review

    The young person moves in with an individual placement plan, reviewed regularly with the local authority and other professionals involved.

Registration, policies and key documents

Information placing authorities and commissioning teams typically need as part of due diligence.

Ofsted registration

Westmere Care Limited is a new provider and has not yet applied for registration as a children's home with Ofsted. We are preparing our application and will publish our registration status, URN and inspection reports here as soon as registration is underway.

Statement of Purpose & Children's Guide

Our Statement of Purpose and Children's Guide are being prepared as part of our registration process. Both will be shared with placing authorities on request and published here once complete.

Complaints procedure

We want to hear about any concern quickly, so it can be put right. In the first instance, a concern can be raised informally with a member of staff or with the home's manager. If it isn't resolved that way, it can be made formally in writing to the manager, who will investigate and respond as quickly as possible. Any complaint that cannot be resolved directly with us can be raised with the placing local authority or, once we are registered, with Ofsted.

Safeguarding & whistleblowing

Safeguarding the children and young people in our care comes first in everything we do. All staff receive safeguarding and safer-recruitment training, and know how to raise a concern about a colleague's conduct through our whistleblowing policy. Full copies of our safeguarding and whistleblowing policies are available to placing authorities and professionals on request.
