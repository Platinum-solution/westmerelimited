Careers

Join our team

We're building a staff team who genuinely want to make a difference in young people's lives. If that sounds like you, we'd like to hear from you.

Why work with us

  • Full paid induction and shadow shifts
  • Support towards the Level 3 Diploma for Residential Childcare
  • Regular supervision and reflective practice
  • A small, supportive team and a workplace pension

What we look for

Every role in our home involves direct contact with children and young people, so safeguarding is at the heart of our recruitment.

Values first

Patience, resilience and a genuine commitment to children's welfare matter more to us than a polished CV alone.

Safer recruitment

All roles are subject to an enhanced DBS check, robust reference checks, and full safeguarding induction training before starting.

Ongoing development

We support and encourage all care staff to work towards a recognised qualification, such as the Level 3 Diploma for Residential Childcare, alongside a full induction, shadow shifts before you start, and ongoing training throughout your time with us.
