Who we are

Westmere Care Limited (Company No. 17308051) is a residential care provider working with local authorities across London and the surrounding region. We support children and young people aged 8 to 17, offering placements ranging from shorter-term arrangements through to longer, more settled placements, depending on each young person's care plan.

A stable, homely environment

Consistent routines, a settled staff team, and a home designed to feel calm and unhurried rather than institutional.

Individualised care planning

Every placement plan is built around the child's own needs, history and goals, and reviewed regularly with the placing authority.

Multi-agency working

Close, transparent communication with social workers, IROs, education providers and health services throughout a placement.

For local authorities and commissioners

We know that placement decisions are made under pressure and against tight timescales. We aim to make it straightforward for social workers and commissioning teams to get the information they need, quickly and clearly, and to be a reliable partner once a placement begins.
