Our commitment to the Children's Homes Quality Standards

Whatever stage of registration we are at, we design and run our home with these nine standards — set out in the Children's Homes (England) Regulations 2015 — at the centre of everything we do.

Quality and purpose of care

A clear statement of purpose that matches what we actually provide day to day.

Children's views, wishes and feelings

Genuine opportunities for young people to be heard and to influence decisions that affect them.

Education

Support to attend, engage with and achieve in education, and to plan for the future.

Enjoyment and achievement

Encouragement to take part in activities and interests, and to build on personal strengths.

Health and wellbeing

Timely access to physical and emotional health support, understood in the context of each child's history.

Positive relationships

Consistent, trusted relationships with staff, and support to maintain family and community connections where appropriate.

Protection of children

Robust safeguarding practice, safer recruitment, and a culture where concerns are raised and acted on.

Leadership and management

Effective, qualified management providing clear direction, supervision and accountability.

Care planning

Placement plans that reflect each child's care plan and are reviewed in partnership with the placing authority.
